CareSync — Data Flow Diagrams (DFD)

Visual data flow models illustrating processes, external entities, and data stores.

1. DFD Level 0 — Context Diagram

Context Level

Depicts the overall system context (Process 0.0) interfacing with external entities: Patient, Caregiver, Neon Cloud PostgreSQL Database, Google Gemini AI, and Web Audio Synthesizer.

2. DFD Level 1 — System Process Decomposition

Process Flow

Decomposes the core system into 5 discrete sub-processes and 4 persistent data stores:

  • 1.0 User Auth & Role Guard (interfaces with D1: Users & Profiles)
  • 2.0 Medicine Scheduler & Alarm Engine (interfaces with D2: Medicine Schedule & Logs)
  • 3.0 Vitals Telemetry & Trend Analytics (interfaces with D3: Vitals Readings)
  • 4.0 Gemini AI Nutrition & Bio Engine (interfaces with D4: Nutrition Plans)
  • 5.0 Caregiver Alert & Remote Portal (escalates missed doses & SOS warnings)

3. DFD Level 2 — Medicine Scheduling & Alarm Dispatch Engine

Detailed Pipeline

Details the inner loop of Process 2.0:

  • 2.1 Check Medicine Schedule Time: Timer queries scheduled medicines matching current hour/min.
  • 2.2 Web Audio Frequency Synthesizer & Alarm Modal: Triggers harmonic chimes and modal dialog.
  • 2.3 Record Patient Adherence: Captures Taken, Snoozed, or Skipped action into adherence logs.
  • 2.4 Decrement Inventory Stock & Refill Check: Reduces available stock count and triggers refill alert if < 7 days.
  • 2.5 Dispatch Caregiver SOS Escalation: Notifies caregiver upon skipped or missed doses.
