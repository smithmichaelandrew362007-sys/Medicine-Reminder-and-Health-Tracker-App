====================================================================================
PROJECT SYNOPSIS: CARESYNC - MEDICINE REMINDER AND HEALTH TRACKER APPLICATION
====================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVE:
CareSync is an intelligent healthcare web and desktop application designed to eliminate
medication non-adherence and streamline daily health tracking for patients and caregivers.
It provides automated scheduling, vital sign telemetry, and a remote safety monitoring network.

2. KEY MODULES & CAPABILITIES:
- Smart Medication Management: Visual schedules, dose tracking, and Web Audio melodic chimes.
- Remote Caregiver Telemetry: Live alerts for missed doses, abnormal vitals, and emergency SOS.
- Clinical Vitals Tracking: Interactive logging and charts for BP, blood glucose, weight, and BMI.
- Digital Health Vault: Document archiving for prescriptions, lab results, and pharmacy refills.
- Gemini AI Nutrition Engine: Tailored caloric intake calculations, meal analysis, and diet advice.
- Python Bio-Chronology Engine: Life-span analytics, chronological cohorting, and health screenings.
- Interactive Visual Calendar: Decade jumps, quick age presets, and accessible date of birth picker.

3. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & STACK:
- Client Layer: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 Design Tokens (Light, Dark, Rose, Crimson), Vanilla JS.
- Application Layer: Node.js with Express REST API and Python 3.14 clinical computation service.
- Database Layer: Neon Serverless PostgreSQL with secure relational pooling and ACID compliance.

4. SIGNIFICANCE & OUTCOME:
CareSync empowers patients with proactive wellness habits while granting families peace of mind.
